Tradition & Kasherut

Shabbat Meals Service

Celebrate Shabbat with the authentic taste and warmth of Libyan-Jewish kosher cuisine. Little Tripoli provides a specialized Shabbat Meal Service, available for takeaway and advance pre-orders.

You can pre-order all the beloved classics of our culinary heritage: our signature handmade Cous Cous with rich broth and tender meat, savory stuffed Mafrum, fiery aromatic Hraimi fish, assorted Mediterranean dips (Hummus, Tehina, spiced salads), and traditional festive desserts.

Takeaway & Pickup Carefully packaged and sealed in compliance with kosher standards.
Custom Menus Tailored options for families, individuals, and travelers visiting Rome.
Advance Notice Please place your Shabbat orders in advance (by Thursday morning).

Please contact us by phone or WhatsApp to receive the complete Shabbat menu and arrange your order details.
