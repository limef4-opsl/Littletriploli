Our Story

Tradition, passion, and culinary heritage since 2013

Little Tripoli was born in 2013 from the desire to bring the authentic culinary tradition of the Jewish community of Tripoli to Rome. Located in the vibrant Piazza Bologna area, our restaurant has become a reference point for those seeking the true and intense flavors of Libya.

Ours is a "home-style" cuisine, made of recipes passed down from generation to generation. Each dish tells a story of spices, slow cooking, and simple ingredients that, combined with wisdom, create unforgettable harmonies.

Being a Kosher restaurant means for us not only respect for the rules, but also a guarantee of quality and extreme attention to the choice of every product that enters our kitchen.

Tripoline Cuisine

"Tripoline cuisine is a magical crossroads of Mediterranean, African, and Middle Eastern culinary heritage. It is defined by aromatic spices like cumin, paprika, and caraway, and by iconic dishes such as hand-rolled Cous Cous and Hraimi, the famous fish simmered in a rich spiced sauce."
